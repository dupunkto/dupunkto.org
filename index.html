Improving the world line by line, using code.

{du}punkto is a small hacker* collective consisting of four teenagers (Robin, Pot, Gijs, and Mika) from the Netherlands. We aspire to make a positive impact on the world by writing great software.

On this site, we host some of our projects, our wiki, our repos and services needed to develop and maintain all that.

Current research

Somtomorrow, a prototype next-gen cross-platform Somtoday client that's actually good.

Scatterpubb, a yet-to-be-named project that explores ways to blend websites, social media and collaboration on the Web, by implementing an easy-to-use website engine powered by various open standards.

Other projects

Gitz, an awesome Git frontend written in PHP.

Cipher, a tool for securely sending and receiving passphrases and API keys.

Tools, useful web-based tools for everyday (development or design) tasks.

Past projects

Oblikvo, a browser-based 3D first-person shooter with old school graphics and multiplayer support.

Kodo, a game that's under 2kb so it fits in a QR code; also its all in one url.

Linio, a local-first note system centered around plain text files.
